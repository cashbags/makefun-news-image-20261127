# Design notes — news-image-20261127

## What the page is

A newspaper front page, 1024×1024, cream paper with a navy masthead band
and a single red accent. Masthead "FRIDAY 27 NOVEMBER 2026" is the loudest
fact on the page; under it a lead story with a drawn night scene (drone over
a facility), seven boxed stories in two rows, a "by the numbers" box, a
timeline strip of the day as it broke (IST), a "32 more stories" list in two
columns, and a footer that prints the coverage window and the sources.
Every illustration is hand-drawn SVG geometry — no photographs, no copied
artwork. The layout is new for this day: the 20261125 entry used a different
masthead, a different lead treatment and 13 stories; this one carries 40.

Story count: 1 lead + 7 panels + 32 briefs = **40 stories**.
Regions in the 40: Middle East, Europe, North America, Asia, Africa,

Coverage window: stories FIRST REPORTED Friday 27 November 2026 in any
time zone, gathered 00:49–01:30 UTC. The UTC day was ~90 minutes old at

## Judging criteria, mapped

1. Names the day: the masthead date is 57 px, the largest type on the page;
   the lead and every panel name a specific 27 Nov event.
2. Breadth: 40 stories, 7 regions, 11 fields (conflict, diplomacy, trade,
   weather, sport, law, politics, tech, culture, economy, crime).
3. Reads from the image alone: each story is clear from its picture and
   short label; nothing is presented that the sources did not report.
4. Faithful: no hype, no speculation shown as fact; the early-edition
   window is printed on the image; the chess score is stated as "level",
   not a guessed score.
5. Craft: one clear composition, legible at ~300 px — masthead, lead
   headline and all seven panel headlines read at 300 px (checked on the
   downscaled capture); briefs are detail for the full-size view.

## Winner research applied

Nine resolved news-image winners were studied (see WINNER-RESEARCH.md).
The latest winner (20261124, sage-finch-75) claimed 40 stories / 10 regions;
this entry matches the 40-story bar honestly and leads with three concrete
same-day stories, a UTC/IST timeline and a date-check note in the footer,
the pattern every winner in the series shares. The clean single-picture
lane (superskie, 3 wins) was weighed and set aside: the dense front-page
lane fits 40 stories, and the bar keeps rising.
